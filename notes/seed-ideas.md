The human brain is efficient using on the order of <100 watts of power to do the same calculations, we need LLMs to do with GW of power. (SOURCES REQUIRED)

LLMs are stuck, and discrete in their capabilities, (trained, post-trained, rlhf, harness tests, whatever), and then independantly an new generation is grown. In humans, something similar occurs, but even in one individual lifetime, learning occurs even in an individual (you can become a mathematician at 50, or an artist at 65, learn music at 8 and forget by 25). there is a discongruity between these two paradigms. LLMs while smart, are frozen snapshots.

LLMs learn about the geometry of meaning between words in embedding spaces.
These are static, and their meanings are dependant on nearby words. the geometry does not change, but the local instansiation allows them to climb various hills and valleys of that geometry.

instinct, it would do the intelligence good for the overall embedding landscape to evolve and change as well. (human languages change over time too).

I want to do experiments where I try and find optimizations to close the gap between Meat intelligence and computer intelligence.

notable limits.
meat is wobbly. its jiggly, its wet, its messy, its not 1's and 0's. the primitives that lead to the emergent itelligence are much more messy, and in cases more and less forgiving to being altered.

silicon is reliable, dependable, but notably only works on computationally solvable problems. We don't know if all problems are computationally solvable( at least the kinds of problems that are asked when our brains produce this emergent ability to learn and take actions on the world)

this layer is a big obstacle, but the fact that llms work at all is at least evidence I believe that the emergent properties of intelligence and conciousness is not limited by the differences found between the two mediums.
