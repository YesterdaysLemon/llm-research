## Shared verdict

The affine effect is real as a training result, but the manuscript's preferred mechanism was too specific.

Relational teacher geometry improved held-out-pair accuracy by 12.01 percentage points over output distillation across ten paired seeds. The input-to-target correspondence matters, and random or untrained features do not reproduce the learned-teacher result. However, the experiment did not include the simplest teacher-free alternative: a Gram target in which examples are similar exactly when their terminal labels are equal. The present evidence therefore does not distinguish teacher-specific internal geometry from supervised answer-similarity or metric-learning structure.

Both reviewers also rejected the phrase “transferred local structure but not a portable program” as a settled mechanistic conclusion. The relational student did not merely fail out of distribution at depth four; its mean in-distribution depth-four accuracy was only 8.15%. That student had not demonstrated possession of a depth-four algorithm that could then fail to transfer. The registered strong-algorithm criterion was genuinely not supported, but the cause remains unresolved among capacity, optimization, architecture, and the information content of the target.

## Verified quantitative reanalysis

The original paper reported only the held-out depth curve. The council prompted a second look at in-distribution accuracy by depth. Define the descriptive normalized-transfer ratio

```text
rho_d = (heldout_d - chance) / (in_distribution_d - chance).
```

For the affine logits student, mean `rho` at depths two, three, and four is 0.445, 0.127, and 0.076. For the relational student it is 0.793, 0.341, and 0.230. Relational supervision therefore improves held-out composition even conditional on the amount of above-chance in-distribution behavior. This strengthens the behavioral transfer result while weakening the previous claim that the remaining depth failure identifies a non-portable algorithm.

The corresponding mean in-distribution accuracies are:

| Condition | Overall | Depth 2 | Depth 3 | Depth 4 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Labels | 60.84% | 89.86% | 27.19% | 5.17% |
| Logits | 61.77% | 91.09% | 29.49% | 5.54% |
| Relational | 68.55% | 97.00% | 49.87% | 8.15% |

These are descriptive reanalyses of frozen results, not newly registered endpoints.

## Opus 5 contributions

Opus identified four issues that materially changed the interpretation:

1. The target is layer-indexed representation geometry, not a trajectory indexed by task-computation steps. The two student states come from encoder layers; the matched teacher states are layers zero and five. The final teacher state is also the classifier input and may contain answer-class structure.
2. The omitted target-label Gram is the strongest missing control. It can separate activation-specific information from a teacher-free class-similarity objective.
3. The shuffled and random controls are not neutral. Per-minibatch shuffling supplies a changing wrong correspondence, while normalized random features encourage a different geometry. Their poor performance can overstate the evidential force of relational-minus-shuffled. Relational-minus-logits remains the clean primary effect.
4. E001-P1's 80% capability gate was not transferred to the generic Transformer in E002. Reporting E002 in-distribution depth accuracy is therefore essential to interpreting its depth failure.

Opus proposed a five-condition tiny language-model study: ordinary next-token learning, output distillation, output plus learned hidden relations, output plus target-derived relations, and an extra-data or extra-compute baseline. It recommended a natural-language validation stream plus a small controlled compositional stream, with student-capability gates fixed before interpreting extrapolation.

## Qwen 3.8 contributions

Qwen independently emphasized three additional boundaries:

1. Relational caches are much larger than logit caches (approximately 40.96 MB versus 3.76 MB in affine), and the earlier data-rich and wall-time-matched ordinary-supervision controls were never reached after E001-P1 failed its capability gate. The present study is not an information- or compute-matched efficiency result.
2. The transition-table executor establishes that an exact compact factorization exists, but not that context selection caused the advantage. It receives the state space, relation inventory, and recurrence rule. A useful next architecture would learn a soft router or latent operator library rather than receiving this decomposition.
3. Spectrum-preserving, byte-matched, fixed-permutation, longer-budget, and weight-tied recurrent controls would help distinguish geometric correspondence from regularization and optimization effects.

Qwen proposed a controlled “walk language” next-token task with a small natural-text anchor so that causal language modeling can be tested before committing to a large corpus.

## Rejected or qualified reviewer claims

One Qwen implementation claim did not survive local verification. The per-minibatch shuffled condition calls `torch.randperm` without an explicit generator at that line, but every training run first calls the shared `set_seed`, which seeds PyTorch's global RNG. The condition is therefore reproducible for a given run seed. It is still a changing per-minibatch wrong correspondence, not a single frozen permutation; that is the scientifically relevant limitation.

The council's runtime estimates for a tiny language-model study are planning estimates, not measurements. They must not be treated as a compute or energy result.

## Decisions caused by the council

1. Narrow the paper's principal conclusion to a reproducible correspondence-preserving learned-teacher geometry effect whose teacher-specific content is unresolved.
2. Report in-distribution depth accuracy and the descriptive normalized-transfer analysis.
3. Treat the executor's 61.3-fold stored-parameter ratio as a two-model existence comparison, not a swept frontier or a causal conditional-computation result.
4. Describe selected table parameters as a semantic property of the factorization. The current implementation computes a softmax for every table before indexing, so its wall-clock forward pass does not realize sparse 2,209-parameter execution.
5. Run E003, the predeclared target-label geometry control, before scaling the objective to a tiny causal language model.
6. Carry target-derived geometry and ordinary extra-compute/data baselines into the tiny-language-model design.
